Marta: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Marta is a private assistant used only by its owner, Nawaf Felemban, and his household. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What data Marta accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, Marta uses the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) on the owner's own Google account. It reads documents that the owner sends to it and files, renames and moves those documents within the owner's Drive, with an undo record.

How the data is used

  • Only to provide Marta's single function: filing and organizing the owner's own documents and answering the owner's questions about them.
  • Document text may be processed by third-party AI model providers chosen by the owner, under privacy rules that limit what leaves the owner's server. It is not used for advertising, and is not used to train models.

Storage and security

Marta runs on a private server reachable only over the owner's private network. Google credentials and job records are stored there with restricted file permissions. Job records are backed up over the private network to the owner's own storage.

Sharing

Marta does not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, and no person other than the owner and household members sees it, except where required by law.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Marta's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as needed for the functions above. The owner can revoke Marta's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored credentials and records from the server.
